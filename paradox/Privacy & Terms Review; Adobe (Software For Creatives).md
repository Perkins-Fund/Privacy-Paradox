# DATA RETENTION

Adobe process and keep most personal information for as long as the user is an active user of their services.

If a user chooses to delete their account, they delete *“certain personal information”*, retaining what they need to to comply with business and legal requirements (i.e. financial transactions/purchases), which they retain for ten years after the users last interaction with them.

Photos’ and ID submitted for identity verification is deleted by the third party identity verification provider within 180 days of collection.

# WHAT IS DISCLOSED AND WITH WHO

Adobe state that they will disclose personal information within the Adobe family of global companies, including acquired companies (you can [view those here](https://www.adobe.com/uk/about-adobe/contact/offices.html)).

They state they also disclose personal information;

* With other third-party data controllers (in order to provide their services)  
* Resellers  
* Sales and advertising partners  
* Retailers  
* Advertisers  
* Advertising agencies  
* Advertising networks and platforms  
* Information service providers  
* Fraud monitoring and prevention providers  
* Social media platforms  
* Government authorities

Adobe state they Audience Match Partners as;

* Meta  
* Pinterest  
* Snap  
* TikTok  
* Amazon  
* Quantcast  
* Yahoo  
* Google

Sharing user information with the above companies supports cross-platform targeted advertising.

Where the users Adobe account is associated with a business domain/business email address, they may provide user personal information to that business.

Where a user uploads content to Creative Cloud and Document Cloud apps, Adobe *“may analyse the content processed or stored on their servers using techniques such as machine learning to improve our products and services”.*

*“Adobe does not analyse your content to train generative AI models, unless you choose to submit content to the Adobe Stock marketplace”.*

Adobe state they do not use student data (this does not include universities, they collect University data) to inform or direct targeted online advertising or marketing to students.

If a user signs into an Adobe service using a third party login, certain information is shared with and received from the third party.

# TERMS OF SERVICE

Adobe products are not intended for users under the age of 13, except certain products (Project Aqua).

User agrees that Adobe can review content on their servers for illegal content, abusive content or behaviours.

Locally stored content on a users device is not scanned or reviewed.

User is granted a licence to use Adobe software.

Users have thirty (30) days from the point of terminating their account and/or licence to download any content stored in the Services.

Adobe offer a paid *“Talent Search”* feature to recruiters and companies looking to discover and hire creative talents, by uploading a public profile or public project on the services, the user agrees that the public information may be included in Talent Search results.

Content a user submits to Adobe Stock Marketplace is used to train generative AI models, the user grants Adobe a licence to do this in the act of submitting the content.
