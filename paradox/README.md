# Privacy Paradox

Privacy is a paradox

As our world becomes digitized increasingly further, it's more important now than ever for individuals (as well as corporations and enterprise users) to understand the trade off at play for personal and sensitive data including digital assets when using online products and services.

Abandoning privacy should not be a prerequisite for achieving security, nor should control of personal data be given up for but a small convenience when the wider implications of which can be significant given the cyber security community is continually seeing a record level of data breaches and data leaks occurring globally.

The collection of articles here are designed to give any reader an understanding in simplified terms for a variety of web and app based products and digital service;

* What data is being collected
* How long the data is retained for
* What data is disclosed and with who
* Terms of Service clauses you should be aware of
* Cookies deployed
* Other considerations

Articles have been formed from full (non-AI, human only) analysis of the products terms and policies.
